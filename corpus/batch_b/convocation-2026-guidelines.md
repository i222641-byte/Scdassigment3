# Convocation 2026 Guidelines for Graduates

Batch B test document. Written by the group to test ingestion; it is not an official
FAST-NUCES notice.

## Date and reporting time

The Convocation 2026 ceremony for the Islamabad campus will take place on Saturday
19 December 2026. Graduates must report at 8:30 am for the rehearsal; the ceremony begins
at 11:00 am.

## Academic gown

Graduates collect their academic gown from the Student Affairs office between
14 and 17 December 2026 after paying the gown charges of Rs 4,500. The gown must be returned
on the same day after the ceremony, otherwise the security deposit refund is withheld.

## Guests

Each graduate receives two guest passes. Children under ten years of age are not allowed in
the hall. Guests must be seated by 10:30 am.

## Eligibility

Only graduates whose degree requirements are complete and whose dues are cleared by
30 November 2026 are eligible to receive their degree at the ceremony.
