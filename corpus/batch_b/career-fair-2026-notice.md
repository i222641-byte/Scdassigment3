# Islamabad Campus Career Fair 2026

Batch B test document. Written by the group to test ingestion; it is not an official
FAST-NUCES notice.

## Date and venue

The Career Fair 2026 will be held on Thursday 10 December 2026 from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm
in the main auditorium and the adjoining lawn of the Islamabad campus. Forty-five employers
have confirmed participation, including software houses, banks and telecom operators.

## Who can attend

The fair is open to final-year (eighth semester) students and to graduates of the last two
batches. Seventh-semester students may attend the afternoon session from 2:00 pm onwards.

## Registration

Students must register through the Career Services Office (CSO) form before
3 December 2026. Registered students receive a QR pass by email; walk-in entry without a
pass is not allowed. Each student may submit their CV to at most ten employers through the
CSO portal before the fair.

## Dress code and conduct

Formal dress is required. Students must carry their university ID card and at least five
printed copies of their CV.
